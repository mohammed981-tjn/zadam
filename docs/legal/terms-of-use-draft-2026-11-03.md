## النسخة العربيّة

### ١) من نحن

«سوداقري» منصّة رقميّة للزراعة والثروة الحيوانيّة في السودان، تقدّم معلومات وأدوات وخدمات للمنتجين والمستثمرين ومقدّمي الخدمة. يديرها `«سوداقري»` [الاسم القانونيّ للجهة المشغّلة، ورقم سجلّها، وعنوانها: يُكمَل بعد التسجيل].

في هذه الشروط:
- **«المنصّة»**: موقع سوداقري وتطبيقه وما يتّصل بهما.
- **«نحن»**: الجهة المشغّلة للمنصّة.
- **«أنت» أو «المستخدم»**: كلّ من يزور المنصّة أو يسجّل فيها، مزارعاً كان أو مستثمراً أو مقدّم خدمة أو شركة أو مشترياً.

- يجب أن تكون قد بلغت ١٨ سنة، أو السنّ القانونيّة للتعاقد في بلدك إن كانت أعلى.
- إن سجّلت باسم شركة أو جمعيّة فأنت تقرّ بأنّ لك صلاحيّة إلزامها بهذه الشروط.

- **ما تقدّمه:** المنصّة وسيط معلومات وخدمات. تعرض الأراضي والفرص والأسعار والأدوات، وتُيسّر كتابة الاتّفاقات بين المستخدمين وتوقيعها وتوثيق مراحلها بالأدلّة.

- **لا ضمانات:** المنصّة لا تضمن ربحاً ولا عائداً ولا محصولاً ولا سعراً ولا جودة خدمة.

### ٥) الأراضي والتوثيق

- **صدق المعلومات:** يقرّ صاحب الأرض بأنّ ما يسجّله عنها صحيح، ومنه موقعها ومساحتها وحيازتها ومستنداتها، وبأنّ له حقّ عرضها.

### 1) Who we are

Sudagri is a digital platform for agriculture and livestock in Sudan, providing information, tools and services to producers, investors and service providers. It is operated by `«Sudagri»` [the operator's legal name, registration number and address — to be completed once registered].

- **"the platform"** means Sudagri's website and app and what connects to them;
- **"we"** means the operator;
- **"you" or "the user"** means anyone who visits or registers, whether farmer, investor, service provider, company or buyer.

### 2) Accepting these terms

- By using or registering on the platform you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.
- If you do not agree, do not use the platform.
- You must be at least 18, or the legal age to contract where you live if that is higher.
- If you register for a company or cooperative, you confirm you have authority to bind it.

### 3) Your account

- **Accurate details:** You register with your phone number, email or Google account, and keep your details accurate and up to date.
- **Security:** You are responsible for keeping your password secret and for everything done from your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has entered it.
- **One account:** One account per person, unless we allow otherwise.
- **Agents:** A verified cooperative or company may act for a farmer under a recorded mandate that the farmer accepts from their own account, and only within what it covers. Everything an agent does is recorded in the farmer's name.
- **Closing your account:** You may ask to close your account from your account page. Records in which another party or the law has a right are kept, such as signed contracts and their entries, as the privacy policy explains.

### 4) The platform's role

- **What it does:** The platform is an information and services intermediary. It shows lands, opportunities, prices and tools, and helps users write and sign agreements between them and record their stages with evidence.
- **Not a party:** It is not a party to agreements between users, such as partnership agreements, forward contracts and service contracts, unless a contract expressly names a service the platform itself provides, such as supervision.
- **Witnessing a contract:** When the platform "witnesses" a contract, it keeps its text, its parties' signatures, its digital fingerprint and the dates. This is not a guarantee that the contract will be performed.
- **No money held:** The platform does not receive, hold or transfer users' money. Payments are made directly between the parties by the means they agree. Payments the platform records are a record of what both parties confirmed, not a transfer by the platform.
- **No guarantees:** The platform guarantees no profit, return, yield, price or quality of service.

### 5) Lands and verification

- **Accurate information:** A landowner confirms that what they record about their land is true, including its location, area, tenure and documents, and that they have the right to offer it.
- **What "verified land" means:** A platform supervisor opened its documents and reviewed them for completeness and consistency, and who verified it, when and at what area are recorded. It is not a ruling on, or guarantee of, ownership, and does not replace an investor's own checks or an inspection.
- **Changed details:** If a verified land's details change, such as its area, boundary or location, it goes back to review, and no new partnership can be signed on it until it is verified again.
- **The paid inspection report:** It describes what the inspector saw on the day of the visit, for a fee announced before you request it. It is not a guarantee of the land's condition afterwards.

### 6) Investment and partnerships

- **Not investment advice:** The opportunities, feasibility studies, trust scores and risk assessments the platform shows are information to help your decision, not investment advice.
- **Risks:** Farming is exposed to weather, pests, prices, and security and economic conditions, and you may lose part or all of your money.
- **Acknowledging the risk:** Before expressing interest in any land or opportunity, you are asked to confirm that you have read the risk notice.
- **Partnership agreements:** Written by the parties on the platform, whether sharecropping, lease, finance, musaqah or a company-managed partnership. Their text is frozen at signature, and they count against the verified area. Their parties alone are responsible for performing them.
- **[Direct investment in projects through the platform is not enabled. If it is enabled later, its terms will be added after legal advice.]**

### 7) Service contracts

- **Creating one:** The client creates a contract as a draft and offers it to the provider, who accepts it.
- **What freezes:** Once offered, its terms freeze: the parties, season, currency, phases, quantities and prices. Its amount is the sum of its phases.
- **Phases:** The provider starts and delivers each phase. The client approves it after proof, where proof is required, then records its payment. An approved phase does not go back.
- **Deletion and cancellation:** A contract cannot be deleted once offered; it is cancelled or disputed.
- **Disputes:** The administration decides on the evidence both parties provide, and may change the state of phases as far as that requires. `[Is the administration's decision final, or advisory with the parties free to go to court or arbitration? — for counsel]`

### 8) Forward contracts

- **What they are:** A contract between a buyer and a farmer for produce delivered later, at a price and quantity they agree.
- **Penalties:** The parties write any penalty and compensation rates into the contract.
- **Signature and cancellation:** It does not change after signature, and is cancelled only by both parties.
- **Delivery:** The buyer records delivery and the farmer confirms it.
- **Disputes:** The administration decides as in section 7. `[For counsel: are such penalties valid and enforceable under Sudanese law?]`

### 9) Fees

- **Free:** Registration and the general tools are free, including the water calculator, feasibility, prices and the assistant.
- **Supervision and service fees:** Written into the contract itself before signature.
- **Other platform fees:** Proposed by an administrator, and recorded against no one before the payer confirms them.
- **The inspection report fee:** Announced before you request the report.
- **Taxes:** Each party is responsible for their own taxes and legal obligations on what they earn or pay.

### 10) Information and tools

- **Sources and accuracy:** Prices, exchange rates, weather, soil, water, estimates and agricultural knowledge come from published public sources or the platform's own calculations, with the source and date shown where possible. They may be out of date, incomplete or wrong.
- **Not a substitute for experts:** They do not replace the advice of an agronomist, a veterinarian, or a financial or legal adviser.
- **The AI assistant** is an automated program that can be wrong. It says when it answers from the platform's knowledge base and when from general knowledge. Do not rely on its answer alone for a decision with financial or health consequences.

### 11) What you upload and publish

- **What you confirm:** That what you upload is true and that you have the right to upload it, including photos, documents, evidence, messages, prices and offers.
- **Your permission to us:** You allow the platform to use it as far as needed to provide the service and keep the record, such as showing it to the other party to a contract and keeping it as evidence.

### 12) Privacy

- **The policy:** Your data is handled under the [privacy policy](/privacy).
- **Downloading your data:** You may download a copy of your data from your account page.
- **What is shown on acceptance:** A land's exact location, its area and its owner's phone are shown to an investor or company only after the owner accepts their request.

### 13) The administration's powers

The administration may:

- hide or remove content that breaches these terms;
